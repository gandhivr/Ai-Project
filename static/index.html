Why Haar Features Degrade Under Face Masks (Condition E)

Haar Cascades compute rectangular intensity sum differences over integral images. The primary cascade filters mandate: (1) Eye-Nose horizontal edge, (2) Nose-Lip vertical contrast, and (3) Oral-mental sulcus horizontal dark-over-light mouth edge. Occluding 45% of the lower face physically eradicates features #2 and #3, causing early decision-tree stages to prematurely drop candidate windows.
